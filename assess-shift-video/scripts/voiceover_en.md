## Direction
- Voice: calm, curious, practical. A thoughtful career partner, not an advert.
- Pronounce **ASSESS SHIFT** as two clear words. Package names are proper nouns: no emphasis that suggests ranking.
- Leave a short breath after lines 1, 3, 4 and 9.
- Line 6: "Personality insight is included in selected journeys" is a factual qualifier (Career Direction and Career Transformation only). Keep it neutral.

## Claims check (keep these out of any rewrite)
