# ASSESS SHIFT motion playbook

Status: proposed character performance rules plus a checked working vector study. Update this file with accepted render corrections; do not describe unreviewed choices as approved.

One main action per shot. Gaze establishes attention. Arm movement follows shoulder, elbow and wrist pivots. The listener reacts after the initiating cue. Meaningful holds give the audience time to understand. Keep feet fixed in standing conversational shots, props attached after contact and text still while reading.

Use the starting timing ranges in CHARACTER_MOTION_BIBLE.md. Keep action timing editable; the narrator’s phrasing determines the final cut. Stagger cards by a modest 4–6 frames, reveal the comparison before highlighting a direction and preserve a card or path across adjacent shots where useful.
