# Practice: Your next move, with clarity

Build a 12-second silent motion-graphics draft that demonstrates character consistency, readable text, card choreography and a coherent transition. This is a proposed ASSESS SHIFT scene, not a reconstruction of an observed UpSend sequence.

## Targets

- Composition: `CareerCoachingPractice`.
- Vertical: 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 360 frames.
- Optional landscape adaptation after vertical review: 1920 × 1080 with reflowed layout.
- Ivory background; burgundy highlights; charcoal text; soft rose cards.
- Samir and Nour are both coaches. Keep matched size and comparable prominence.
- No voiceover or music required. Export a clearly named silent draft.

## Timeline

Frame ranges are start-inclusive and end-exclusive. All copy is creative campaign copy, not a quote from the reference.

## Assets and deliverables

Use separate approved coach poses when available. If only the combined sheet exists, use clearly marked coach placeholders and explain the missing pose assets; do not crop one sheet and present it as a complete layered rig.

Create editable source, central motion/style tokens, timeline data, four key-frame previews and `career-coaching-practice-silent.mp4` if rendering is possible. Include the actual commands and a short review note. If rendering fails, describe the concrete failure and provide the functioning previews and source without claiming an export.

## What the practice should teach

1. The same brand and characters survive every shot.
2. Movement helps explain the relationship between insights and options.
3. Headlines remain legible during animation.
4. Scene transitions preserve a visual connection.
5. Feedback can change one shared timing token and improve multiple scenes consistently.
