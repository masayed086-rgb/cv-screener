# Character roles in the introduction video

Both characters are career coaches. These are creative presentation assignments, not a claim that they have different qualifications. Either coach can listen, ask questions, explore insights and support a plan.

| Character | Primary role in this video | What the viewer sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nour | Career reflection and exploration | Recognizes the opening career question; invites comparison of options with experience, market and life priorities |
| Samir | Assessment insight and transition planning | Presents interests and motivation; connects insight with experience; explains practical steps and testing an option |
| Both | Service introduction and invitation | Present ASSESS SHIFT, package choices and the Chemistry Session with equal professional presence |

## Scene responsibilities at 30 fps

The 75-second timeline is provisional and must follow the final recording. Frame ranges include the start and exclude the end.

| Time | Frames | Lead | Action and pose | Relationship to motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00–07 | 0–210 | Nour | Attentive standing or seated pose beside the opening question | Gentle entrance; text settles while Nour remains still |
| 07–16 | 210–480 | Graphics | Three neutral options: role, field, way of working | Staggered card reveal; no coach chooses for the viewer |
| 16–25 | 480–750 | Both | Matched portraits introduce ASSESS SHIFT | Equal portrait scale; burgundy path connects to the service |
| 25–38 | 750–1140 | Samir | Introduces interests and motivation, then listens | Cards reveal one at a time; Samir's pose changes only when it supports the message |
| 38–51 | 1140–1530 | Nour | Invites option comparison against experience, market and life priorities | Cards align in a comparison; criteria reveal in order |
| 51–61 | 1530–1830 | Samir | Presents manageable next steps and testing an option | Path draws into milestones; each step gets a reading hold |
| 61–68 | 1830–2040 | Both | Frame three packages without diagnosing the viewer | Equal card treatment; no automatic 'best package' selection |
| 68–75 | 2040–2250 | Both | Invite a Chemistry Session | Final layout stays stable for at least three seconds; real URL only when supplied |

Nour has 20 seconds of solo scenes, Samir 23, and both share 23 seconds. The remaining 9 seconds use graphics. Match visible scale, lighting, hierarchy and authority. This plan intentionally uses only the two coaches; the viewer is the implied client.

## Voice roles

Use one external narrator for the initial version. The visual lead does not need to speak on screen. Keep mouths still. If alternating voices are requested, give Nour and Samir distinct reviewed recordings and assign lines according to the scene table. Add lip sync only with the necessary mouth/rig assets or checked character-video segments.

## Emotional progression

Opening: attentive curiosity. Insight: thoughtful interest. Comparison: calm exploration. Planning: practical encouragement. Closing: warm invitation. Avoid worry, diagnosis, pressure to resign or celebration of a guaranteed outcome.
