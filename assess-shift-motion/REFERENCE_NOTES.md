## What was verified

- The linked page identifies the UpSend explainer by What a Story. The player reported a 70-second duration, but playback did not load in this session.
- The creator's case study documents script development, storyboard, stylescape, design, voiceover, animation and sound mixing. It describes floating dashboards and animated dollar symbols.
- The creator's Behance project lists Adobe After Effects and Adobe Illustrator as tools. This establishes the original production tools; it does not mean Claude Code has those tools available.
- Claude Code's official documentation describes CLAUDE.md as persistent project context. Instructions guide behaviour rather than guarantee it.
- Remotion's official documentation describes React compositions and frame-driven animation. It is the proposed implementation route for a new code-based project, not a claim about how the original video was coded.

## What remains unverified

The full video was not watched here. Exact shot order, palette, typography, easing curves, transition durations, narration, music and sound timing have not been measured. The motion playbook and practice timeline are creative proposals for career coaching. They must not be labelled as a frame-by-frame analysis of the reference.
