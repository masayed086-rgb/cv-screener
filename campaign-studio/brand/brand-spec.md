# ASSESS SHIFT | Brand direction v1

## Positioning
A thoughtful space to explore your career and leadership, understand your choices and decide a practical next step. Warm, credible, personal, clear. Address experienced professionals, people navigating a career decision, and emerging leaders. Show realistic uncertainty without diagnosing or shaming anyone.

**Campaign line:** Your next step starts with clarity.
**Arabic:** خطوتك الجاية تبدأ بوضوح.
**First-step CTA:** Book a Chemistry Session.
**Arabic CTA:** احجز جلسة تعارف.
For longer copy: “A Chemistry Session helps us explore your goals, expectations and whether working together is a good fit.” Do not promise results or imply this introductory conversation completes the coaching process.

## Palette (authoritative)
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | #142C3E | Brand, headings, outline strokes, dark scenes |
| Ivory | #F7F3EA | Main background and inverse type |
| Teal | #217A78 | Chosen paths, reflection, buttons |
| Amber | #D9A441 | A decision node or one focal accent |
| Ink | #253642 | Body copy |

Start with 65% ivory, 25% navy, 8% teal and 2% amber as composition guidance, not literal image area requirements. Character skin/hair colors are additional illustration colors. Amber is an accent; use navy type on amber. Avoid ivory body text on amber. Verify contrast for each final text/background pair.

## Typography
- English: Manrope, 700 headlines / 500 labels / 400 body.
- Arabic: Noto Sans Arabic, 700 headlines / 500 body.
- Acquire licensed font files and bundle local webfonts when building videos. These fonts are recommendations, not included assets. PDF uses a separate embedded reading font.
- At 1080 x 1920 start with 76-104 px short headlines, 48-58 px supporting text and 44-52 px captions; inspect at phone size. Arabic may need more line height and shorter lines.
- Headlines: usually 3-7 words; one message at a time. No long bilingual paragraphs inside a reel. Export separate Arabic and English edits.

## Identity and layout
Proposed wordmark: ASSESS above SHIFT, with a small amber forward step beside SHIFT. Supplied logo SVG is a concept and uses editable text.
Signature device: an irregular navy line becomes a deliberate teal path with one amber decision point. Reuse its actual geometry across scenes. It means exploration and choice, not guaranteed upward success.
Quiet ivory space, restrained surfaces, mature character illustration. Avoid generic rockets, gears, floating particles, confetti and neon. Coaching scenes may include a table, notebook and simple plant; keep props secondary.
At 1080 x 1920 keep critical copy initially within x=90..900 and y=200..1520. These are conservative starting guides; check current platform overlays for the actual placement before launch. End card holds long enough to read, usually 3-4 seconds.

## Service descriptions
Career Coaching: Explore what matters, consider possibilities and decide your next step.
Leadership Coaching: Reflect on how you lead and choose behaviors to practice.
Career Advising: Discuss practical career options and recommendations informed by your situation.
Arabic: كوتشينج مهني / كوتشينج قيادي / إرشاد مهني.
Show advising recommendations clearly when that service is being depicted; do not blur them with nondirective coaching.

## Copy style
Specific, conversational, calm. Ask “What matters in your next role?” rather than “Unlock your unlimited potential.” Use “Explore” and “Choose” rather than guaranteed transformation. Egyptian Arabic can feel natural and professional. Do not invent ICF credentials, accreditation, client stories, testimonials, package pricing or evidence of outcomes.
